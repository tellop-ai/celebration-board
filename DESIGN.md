# Celebration board

Approved 2026-10-28: the warm celebration board with a choice of six color palettes per board. Rose is the
starting look; a person may choose Claret, Sage and ivory, Warm linen, Midnight blue, Rose or Lavender.
The approved design shows a compact board header, a centered title and introduction, signed message cards,
a palette chooser, a board picker, and a focused presentation. The message editor shows a live preview beside
its fields when room permits. Wedding and farewell occasions have suitable writing prompts. A person can
change the colors on one board without changing the others.

The palette contracts are generated from Tellop's curated looks and the approved color choices. The locally
shipped Outfit, Inter and Lora fonts supply headings, controls and messages. Start light. The approved design
includes a visitor-pressed dark switch, only on html. The same palette travels to dialogs and presentation.
Both languages fit without clipped controls. Three cards show across a wide window, two at a medium width,
one on a phone. Reading layout centers one wider card per row. Content decides each card's height.

Daily management uses PageFrame, CountsLine, AppMenu and DataTable. Message forms use Dialog with a bounded
height, scrollable fields and reachable actions. Personal pictures use PictureField, videos use FileUpload.
Slideshow pictures use contain; no autoplay sound or motion. AI drafts are separate proposals until accepted.
There is no invite control: people come in through Tellop's own door. Printing still prepares every visible
message before it prints and never includes hidden or waiting messages or an open form.
